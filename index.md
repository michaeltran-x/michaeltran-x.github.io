## PhD Student in Economics

I am a PhD Student in Economics at Freie Universität Berlin and Berlin School of Economics. My research interests are topics at the intersection of labor and macroeconomics, with a focus on spatial questions. Additionally,  I am a contributor to the LNRI project and a research fellow of the Kiel Institute.
