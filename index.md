Head TA

<details>
<summary>Course description</summary>
Students are able to quantify and test economic behavioral equations using statistical methods and observed data. They can describe and apply the basic methods of regression analysis, including parameter testing. Through their trained understanding of econometric models, they can also identify the effects of model violations on estimates and tests of regression parameters and develop appropriate solutions. Through the inclusion of a practical computer exercise, students can independently conduct regression analyses and interpret their results meaningfully.
</details>

## Linking National and Regional Inequality (LNRI) {#lnri}

The LNRI Project is an ongoing initiative to provide open and convenient access to an international database of micro statistics on regional income inequality. All statistics are obtained from administrative data on labor and capital incomes collected from social security records, personal income tax returns and/or population censuses. For comparison, the data from each country are harmonized. <br>

You can find the database and more information here: [**regional-inequality.org**](https://regional-inequality.org)
