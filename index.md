I am a PhD Student in Economics at Freie Universität Berlin and Berlin School of Economics. My research interests are topics at the intersection of labor and macroeconomics, with a focus on spatial questions. Additionally,  I am a contributor to the [LNRI project](#lnri) and a [Kiel Institute Junior Fellow](https://www.kielinstitut.de/de/expertinnen-und-experten/michael-tran-xuan/).
